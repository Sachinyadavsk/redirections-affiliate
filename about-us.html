ABOUT SMARTTEST

A simple tool for testing affiliate and tracking links,inspecting redirect chains, and understanding where a tracking URL ultimately leads.

What is SmartTest?

SmartTest is an affiliate and tracking link testing platform designed to make redirect checking easier.

Instead of manually opening a tracking URL and following every redirect, SmartTest presents the redirect journey in an easy-to-read format.

You can enter a tracking link and inspect the sequence of URLs from the initial request through the final destination.
